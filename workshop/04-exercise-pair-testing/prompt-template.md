# Pair Testing Prompt Template

Copy one of the prompts below into your AI tool to generate a simple usability testing task.

---

## Option A: Same Tool Where You Built the Prototype

```
Look at the prototype I built in this project and the problem it solves.

Generate a quick usability test I can run with a partner in 4 minutes:

1. **The role** my partner should play (the maker from my hypothesis), in one sentence
2. **One simple task** — my success sentence turned into a realistic, one-sentence goal (no hints about the UI)
3. **What to observe** — where are they most likely to hesitate?
4. **One follow-up question** — what should I ask them after?
```

---

## Option B: Any AI Tool (new chat)

```
I built a prototype (or have a design direction) and need to test it with a partner. Here's the context:

**The problem I'm solving:**
[FILL IN: Paste your needs hypothesis from Discovery]

**Moment to prototype / success sentence:**
[FILL IN: From Define]

**What the prototype / direction shows:**
[FILL IN: What does the maker see and do?]

Generate a quick usability test I can run with a partner in 4 minutes:

1. **The role** my partner should play (the maker from my hypothesis)
2. **One simple task** — my success sentence as a realistic, one-sentence goal (no hints)
3. **What to observe**
4. **One follow-up question**
```

---

## After the test: improve

```
My tester hesitated at [WHERE] — they said / did [WHAT].
Change only that part of the prototype. Don't touch anything else.
```

---

## If Your Prototype Isn't Working

```
I have a solution idea that isn't fully built yet.

**The problem I'm solving:**
[FILL IN: Needs hypothesis]

**My proposed solution:**
[FILL IN: Direction in 2–3 sentences]

Generate:
1. A simple question: "Does this solution make sense? What questions do you have?"
2. What to listen for in their response
```
